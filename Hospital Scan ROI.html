All rates and costs are examples, not verified figures. The payment rates are 2026 national hospital outpatient (OPPS) rates quoted for a neck MRA, not a brain MRI. Check the brain MRI payment groups for 70551 and 70553 in the CMS addenda before using these numbers. Freestanding centres and private payers are paid differently, and some pay the contrast agent separately. Scanner time only counts as value when the scanner is the bottleneck. Patients who could not have contrast would receive a 70551 scan anyway, so Pixelence adds clinical value there but no extra payment.
